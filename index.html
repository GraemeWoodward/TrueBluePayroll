Why True Blue Payroll.

After years of seeing businesses experiencing chronic payroll headaches, I started True Blue Payroll in August 2026.

Payroll headaches usually start with incorrect payroll software set up.

Too often, businesses are sold products with promises of absolute compliance, full automation and unlimited expert support.

The reality?

Compliance is sometimes murky, automation is never absolute, support is limited, not necessarily expert, often leaving you at the mercy of a chat box.

Strong payroll foundations come with a deep understanding of the product your business is using together with understanding the legal framework it must work within. It is not a two-minute job or a set and forget task. It is a long term commitment to getting it consistently right and treating your payroll software as an evolving system. It must comply with the law which changes, employees who come and go, change their work patterns and management who invent complex remuneration models.

True Blue Payroll helps payroll practitioners and business owners who are tired of chat bots and ticket systems. True Blue Payroll empowers payroll practitioners and business owners who just want to talk to a real person to address their very specific payroll headaches.
