Why True Blue Payroll?

My payroll journey began while helping run a family business. At the time payroll appeared simple enough. Collect the timesheets, process the payroll and distribute the payslips.

That perception changed when it became time to move payroll platforms. What seemed straightforward quickly revealed the complexity behind accurate payroll processing.

Later, taking responsibility for a payroll of more than 600 employees highlighted how important correct setup, legislative compliance and sound payroll processes really are.
