Why True Blue Payroll?

My payroll journey began while helping run a family business. At the time payroll appeared simple enough. Collect the timesheets, process the payroll and distribute the payslips.

That perception changed when it became time to move payroll platforms. What seemed straightforward quickly revealed the complexity behind accurate payroll processing.

Later, taking responsibility for a payroll of more than 600 employees highlighted how important correct setup, legislative compliance and sound payroll processes really are.

At True Blue Payroll we leave no stone unturned, explain outcomes in plain language and focus on practical, compliant solutions.

About Laurence Gay

Driven, positive and highly skilled payroll consultant and subject matter expert with a passion for payroll accuracy, legislative compliance and business excellence.

Payroll became my professional niche in 2019 and remains the perfect blend of legislation, mathematics, technology and people.

Experience spans in-house payroll leadership, payroll bureau processing, payroll audits, remediation projects, software implementation, product ownership and payroll system design.
