Payroll Audit Services

Payroll audits are one of the most effective ways to identify issues before they become expensive compliance problems.

True Blue Payroll provides independent payroll audit services for New Zealand businesses seeking reassurance that payroll systems, payroll practices and payroll outcomes are operating as intended.

Targeted Payroll Audits

Sometimes a business has a specific concern.

  • Average Daily Pay calculations
  • Relevant Daily Pay calculations
  • Leave balances
  • Final pays
  • Allowance configuration
  • Public holiday calculations

A targeted audit focuses on the identified area and provides practical recommendations to address any issues discovered.

Comprehensive Payroll Audits

A comprehensive payroll audit takes a wider view of payroll operations.

  • Business setup review
  • Employee setup review
  • Payroll component review
  • Leave configuration review
  • Payroll process review
  • Reporting and controls review

Compliant payroll outcomes begin with correct setup. Small configuration errors can remain hidden for years and affect every payroll run.

Payroll Compliance Reviews

Reviews can assess compliance with:

  • Holidays Act 2003
  • Employment Relations Act 2000
  • Wages Protection Act 1983
  • Income Tax Act 2007 payroll obligations
  • Parental Leave and Employment Protection Act 1987

Payroll Remediation Support

Where issues are identified, True Blue Payroll can assist with remediation planning, prioritisation and implementation support.

Why Audit?

I am a True Blue audit advocate. Compliant payroll outcomes start with correct business and employee setup. If your payroll hits chronic headaches every pay run, it may be time to hit the audit button.
