Employment Hero Consulting

Many businesses implement a new payroll platform expecting a largely automated and compliant outcome.

The reality is that payroll software is only as good as its configuration.

Employment Hero is a powerful payroll platform, but like any payroll system it requires appropriate business setup, employee configuration, process design and ongoing maintenance to deliver accurate and compliant payroll outcomes.

As a former Product Owner at Employment Hero, I have worked closely with payroll practitioners, implementation consultants and software developers to improve payroll functionality and ensure payroll outcomes align with New Zealand legislation and best practice.

I understand the platform from multiple perspectives:

  • Payroll practitioner
  • Payroll subject matter expert
  • Product owner
  • Payroll software developer liaison
  • Compliance advocate

Services

  • New Employment Hero Implementations: Independent guidance to help ensure business settings are configured appropriately, payroll policies align with system configuration, employee setup is accurate, leave calculations operate as expected, and compliance risks are identified early.
  • Employment Hero Health Check: A structured review of business setup, employee setup, leave configuration, pay components, payroll processes and reporting configuration. Many payroll problems originate from small configuration issues that can remain hidden for years.
  • Payroll Compliance Reviews: Focused reviews covering Holidays Act obligations, leave calculations, Average Daily Pay, Relevant Daily Pay, public holidays, alternative holidays, final pays and employee classification.
  • Payroll Process Optimisation: Reviews of payroll workflows, approval processes, employee onboarding, offboarding, reporting, documentation and internal controls.
  • Specialist Payroll Support: Support for complex final pays, backpay calculations, leave remediation projects, payroll project work, Employment Leave Act readiness and payroll software migration planning.
