Employment Hero Consulting

Many businesses implement a new payroll platform expecting a largely automated and compliant outcome.

The reality is that payroll software is only as good as its configuration.

Employment Hero Payroll is a powerful platform, yet like any payroll system, it requires appropriate business setup, employee configuration, process design and ongoing maintenance to deliver accurate and compliant payroll outcomes.

As a former Product Owner at Employment Hero, I have worked closely with payroll practitioners, implementation consultants and software developers to improve payroll functionality and ensure payroll outcomes align with New Zealand legislation and best practice.

I understand the platform from multiple perspectives:

  • Payroll practitioner
  • Payroll subject matter expert
  • Product owner
  • Payroll software developer liaison
  • Compliance advocate

Services

  • Employment Hero Health Check: You've struggled through the self guided implementation or went through a managed implementation. You are now BAU but still experiencing niggling recurrent issues. You can't get a straight answer or a clear solution. You feel you are not maximising the platform and all these user articles are simply not helping. The Employment Hero Payroll Health Check consists of a structured review of business setup including pay components settings and leave configuration, and employee setup. Many payroll problems originate from small configuration issues that can remain hidden for years.
  • Payroll Compliance Reviews: Focused reviews of Holidays Act 2003 compliance. How does the leave configuration translate in terms of automation of leave entitlements, leave taken conversion, leave rates and pay slip leave balances. Correct business and employee set up are the foundation of compliant payroll calculations. Therefore, a health check will typically be the first step to a compliance review.
  • Specialist Payroll Support: Support for complex final pays, backpay calculations, leave remediation projects, payroll project work, Employment Leave Act readiness and payroll software migration planning.
