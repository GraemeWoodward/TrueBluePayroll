Why True Blue Payroll?

My payroll journey began while helping run a family business. At the time, payroll appeared simple enough. Collect the timesheets, process the payroll, and distribute the payslips.

That perception changed when it became time to move payroll platforms. What I assumed would be a straightforward change quickly revealed the complexity that sits behind accurate payroll processing.

Later, taking responsibility for the payroll of more than 600 employees highlighted just how important correct setup, legislative compliance, sound processes and detailed payroll knowledge really are.

Payroll is about much more than entering hours and pressing a button. Every business has unique requirements, complex employment arrangements and compliance obligations that require careful attention.

At True Blue Payroll we leave no stone unturned. We explain outcomes in plain language, identify risks before they become problems, and help businesses achieve efficient and compliant payroll outcomes that fit their organisation.

About Laurence Gay

Driven, positive and highly skilled payroll consultant and subject matter expert with a passion for numbers, consistent accuracy, business excellence and legal compliance.

Payroll became my professional niche in 2019 and remains the perfect blend of legislation, mathematics, technology and people.

My experience spans in-house payroll leadership, payroll bureau processing, payroll audits, remediation projects, software implementation, product ownership and payroll system design.

Most recently I have worked within payroll software development, leading teams responsible for delivering accurate and compliant payroll outcomes for New Zealand employers.
