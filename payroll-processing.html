Payroll Processing

A good payroll practitioner never forgets what the coalface feels like. Payroll is about people, accuracy and trust.

True Blue Payroll provides payroll processing support for organisations seeking reliable and professional payroll administration.

Clients will typically fall in three categories:

  • You are a small business, your team is growing. Payroll has become more than collecting a few timesheets, entering hours in a system and distributing pay slips. You need a payroll professional you can partner with so you can create space for your core business and have peace of mind that your employees are in good hands.
  • You are a small business and have now outsourced your payroll to a bureau or to an accountant. It’s now been a while but the reality is: You’re not happy with the service. Hours or elements of remuneration are consistently missed, new starters are not started and leavers are not final paid when they should be. You don’t get answers in a timely manner, the person you deal with changes regularly and there seems to be no consistency of process. You are paying for a service but you are actually spending more time asking questions, chasing for answers and fixing issues, than when you were actually processing payroll. In other words, there is no value add to the outsourcing and it’s adding more stress. You need a payroll professional you can partner with so you get real added value: On time accurate processing, straight answers fast when you or your employees have questions.
  • Emergency payroll processing: Your usual payroller is suddenly temporarily unavailable. You have a mild panic moment. Employees must get paid. On time. There is simply no way out of that one. You need a payroll professional to jump in at short notice to take the reins and keep payroll up and running.
