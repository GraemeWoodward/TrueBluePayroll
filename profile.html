Profile

  • Payroll became my professional niche in 2019. It was and still is the perfect blend of legislation, mathematics and people.
  • I learnt payroll from the ground up: Processing and dissecting payroll platforms, progressing towards process improvements, platform customisation to align with legal framework and company policies.
  • The legal framework around payroll means a constant need to refresh knowledge to stay up to date. The upcoming Employment Leave Act will come with interesting challenges.
  • I have proudly been the main point of contact to provide subject matter expertise on all things payroll legislation.
  • As payroll always sits on a mine of data, I have a passion for analytics and adding value to payroll.
  • The tech aspect of payroll is a big part of why payroll is never boring.
  • I enjoy the omnipresent tension that comes with processing payroll.
  • I believe payroll should be a positive experience for all stakeholders as opposed to a major source of anxiety.
  • I enjoy sharing my passion for payroll and as such took the opportunity to present several webinars with CCH Learning and the NZPPA.
