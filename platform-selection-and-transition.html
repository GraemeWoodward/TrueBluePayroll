Payroll Platform Selection and Transition

Selecting a payroll platform is one of the most important payroll decisions a business makes.

Software vendors provide demonstrations, feature comparisons and implementation promises, but businesses often need independent payroll expertise to help evaluate whether a product is genuinely suitable.

Vendor Demonstration Reviews

A software demonstration can look impressive. However, important payroll questions are often hidden behind standard demonstrations.

Independent review can help test:

  • Leave functionality
  • Final pay processing
  • Complex remuneration arrangements
  • Reporting capability
  • Compliance considerations
  • Workflow suitability

Payroll Transition Planning

Moving from one payroll platform to another introduces risk.

Support can include:

  • Migration planning

  • Employee setup validation
  • Leave data validation
  • Go-live readiness assessment
