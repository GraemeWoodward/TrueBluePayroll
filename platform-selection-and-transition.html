Vendor Demonstration Reviews

A software demonstration can look impressive. However, important payroll questions are often hidden behind standard sales pitches.

Independent review can help test:

  • Suitability for your business and your workforce: Do you have mostly salaried 40-hour week employees or a wide spectrum of different work weeks including permanent full and part time irregular employees where the work week cannot be reliably defined.
  • Complex remuneration models: Are you working with true salaried employees (single rate) or with a wide spectrum of differently structured employment agreements or with multiple collective agreements?
  • Leave functionality: Do you have numerous agreed terms that go over and beyond the Holidays Act 2003? Do you have an annual closedown? Do you have a flexible workforce with regular changes of employment status?
  • Final pay processing: How much manual work will you have to do? How much and how will you be able to automate final pay calculations from the standard components of final pay (holiday pay 8%, deduction of annual holidays taken in advance of entitlement, annual holiday entitlement pay out) to the more complex components (public holiday pay out, alternative holiday pay out, redundancy, etc.).
