Employment Leave Act Readiness

What is the Employment Leave Act (ELA)? In short, it may replace the Holidays Act 2003.

Does it have the potential of being a big payroll deal? Yes it does. The ELA fundamentally changes the way leave is accrued and is paid out.

But surely the ELA is the answer to all the problems caused by the Holidays Act 2003 and now payroll will truly just mean clicking the PAY button? Sadly, no. While the ELA addresses some of the current complexities under the Holidays Act 2003, it has created many more in the process. Potentially, we are in for another wild ride of leave legislation. The ride could arguably be smoothed out by some solid guidance by MBIE. This guidance is still to come.

As a business employing people, should I kick start a payroll transformation now to comply with the ELA? No. Though it is now an Act, it will not commence before the 6th of August 2028. As we are still awaiting the final outcome of the 2026 General Election, this piece of legislation could have its wings clipped before we get anywhere near commencement date. Why? Because Labour and the Greens made it clear that they would not support the ELA.

So, should I think about it at all? That would be a good idea, yes. Understanding the fundamentals and the potential impact on your business is a good first toe in the ELA waters.

Should I change anything in my payroll system now? No. The commencement date, i.e. the effective date, is the 6th of August 2028. Until then, the Holidays Act 2003 is still the act to comply with. Beside, payroll software providers are in the same general election waiting game as the rest of us. They are also waiting for MBIE’s guidance specific to payroll software providers scheduled to be released in November 2026.

As a business employing people, is there anything I can do now that would not be a waste of time if the ELA were repealed? Absolutely, yes. The ELA heavily relies on employment agreements to categorise working hours. Therefore, now is a good time to review employment agreements and your remuneration models with the ELA in mind. As employment agreements are also fundamental to the compliance with the Holidays Act 2003, this will not be wasted time.

Where do I start to understand what changes the ELA potentially represent for my business? True Blue Payroll helps businesses understand the fundamentals of the ELA and how it will impact employment agreements without getting bogged down into the minutia.
